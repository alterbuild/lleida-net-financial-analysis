Company profile, ownership and environment

A founder-led, medium-sized SME selling legally valid digital evidence, back to profit after the 2023 crisis

Business

  • Founded in 1995 by Francisco Sapena; listed in Madrid (BME Growth, 2015) and Paris (Euronext Growth, 2018)
  • EU-qualified trust service provider: certified e-mails, notifications, contracts and SMS, plus wholesale SMS traffic
  • 2023 net loss of €2.66m, then a recovery plan (up to 29 job cuts, UK and Costa Rica units closed)

11
Conclusion

Value creation in 2025: yes, but only just, and not yet durable

Turnaround achieved

Margins up, net debt down 22%, ROCE above WACC in 2025 (EVA €308k)

Still fragile

Flat sales, a third of sales in declining wholesale, short-term debt funding long-term assets

Early warning

H1 2026: sales −9%, EBITDA −27%; at that run rate 2026 EVA turns negative

Way forward

Certified-evidence revenue model, long-term financing, rule-based dividends

Proposed roadmap

1

Q4 2026 to Q3 2027

Refinance debt; collections plan; partner pilots; dividend rule

2

Q4 2027 to 2028

Scale the packs partners pay for; EU wallet services; governance

3

2028 onwards

Licensing if a partner commits; first dividend if triggers are met

Each roadmap step needs a partner commitment obtained at the previous one. Source: team analysis.
